# MamaOut — Brand

*v1 · July 2026 · Built from the launch plan + founder interview. Living document.*

## Product name

**MamaOut** — written as one word, capital M and O in text ("MamaOut"), lowercase `mamaout` in the wordmark/logo and handle (`@mamaout.app`). The name is the promise: mama, out of the house.

## Audience

**Primary:** moms on maternity leave with babies 0–12 months in Tel Aviv / Ramat Gan / Gush Dan. Days are shapeless, events are scattered across ten Instagram pages; they want a reason to leave the house *today* in under a minute of searching.

**Secondary:** English-speaking olim/expat moms — underserved by Hebrew-only sources, tight community, high sharing behavior.

She's tired, competent, and allergic to being talked down to. She doesn't need parenting advice; she needs a plan for Tuesday morning.

## Personality

**Empathetic · Welcoming · Fun.**

- Empathetic: we know the fog of month 3. No pressure, no "shoulds."
- Welcoming: first-person, first-name brand. A mom made this, and it shows. Every mom is invited — Hebrew-first, English-first, baby asleep on her chest at 14:00.
- Fun: going out is the treat, not the chore. Light, sunny, a bit celebratory about small wins ("we left the house before noon!").

Voice note: a pinch of wry honesty about maternity-leave life (from the launch plan) keeps the warmth from turning saccharine. Wry is seasoning, not the dish.

## Visual references

- **Soft & pastel** — Peanut / Flo territory: gentle colors, rounded shapes, generous whitespace, airy compositions.
- **Playful illustrated, as accents only** — small hand-drawn doodles (a sun, a squiggle, a star, a stroller wheel-path) sprinkled over otherwise clean layouts. Illustration decorates; it doesn't carry the whole brand.
- Rounded corners everywhere; nothing sharp, nothing gridlocked.

## Colors

Anchor stays in the purple family (continuity with the live app's #863BFF) but softened toward lilac.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — Lilac | softened brand purple | `#A78BFA` |
| Deep — Plum | text, contrast, logo on light | `#4C2A85` |
| Accent — Butter yellow | sun motif, highlights, CTAs | `#FFD97A` |
| Background — Warm cream | default canvas | `#FAF6EF` |
| Background alt — Pale lilac | cards, alternating sections | `#F3EEFF` |

Rules: cream or pale lilac backgrounds, never pure white or saturated purple as a full background. Yellow is the "go outside" color — use it for suns, highlights, and one pop per composition, not everywhere. Max 3 colors per Instagram post.

## Typography direction

**Clean sans for body/UI + warm serif for headlines** — the serif is the editorial, human voice; the sans keeps the app practical.

- Headlines / blog titles / IG hero text: a warm, slightly quirky serif (e.g. **Fraunces** or **Lora**).
- Body, UI, captions: a friendly humanist sans (e.g. **Nunito Sans** or **Assistant**).
- Hebrew: Fraunces has no Hebrew — pair with **Assistant** (sans, Israeli-designed) for Hebrew text and **Noto Serif Hebrew** if a serif is needed. Choose final pairing when bilingual assets start (week 3–4).
- Sentence case everywhere. No ALL CAPS.

## Things to avoid

- **Corporate / techy:** startup gradients, sharp corners, SaaS-blue, glassy mockups, buzzwords ("leverage", "seamless"). MamaOut is a person, not a platform.
- **Parenting-guru tone:** no advice, no milestones, no "5 tips for a better baby", no expert posture. We list what's on; she decides.
- **Pink babyland clichés:** storks, rattles, pacifier icons (implicit in the pastel-but-not-baby palette above).
- Anything that adds pressure: countdowns, FOMO language, "don't miss out." The brand reduces overwhelm; it never manufactures it.
