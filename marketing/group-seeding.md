# Group Seeding: Targets & Posts

*Companion to launch-plan.md · Cadence: 1–2 groups per week, weeks 3–6*

---

## Part 1 — Target groups

Ordered by suggested sequence. Verified = found via search July 2026; membership counts and rules must be checked from inside Facebook before posting.

### Tier 1 — Your own circles (week 3, safest, highest trust)

These aren't searchable — you're already in them or can get invited through friends:

1. **Your birth-month WhatsApp groups** (from tipat halav, hospital ward, or hitkansut). You have three kids = up to three cohorts of moms. The baby-#3 group is the current one; the older two still have moms with new babies.
2. **Neighborhood WhatsApp groups** — yours and your friends'. Ask one friend per neighborhood to forward the message; a forward from a known member beats a post from a stranger.
3. **Gan/מעון parent groups** of your older kids — moms there often have younger babies too.

### Tier 2 — Verified Facebook groups, Hebrew (weeks 3–4)

4. **דיגיתף עיריית תל אביב יפו** — [facebook.com/groups/digitaf.tlv](https://www.facebook.com/groups/digitaf.tlv/) — the municipality's official community for TLV parents of 0–3s. Exactly your audience. It's a muni-run group, so read rules carefully; if direct promo is out, ask the admins — a city program aggregating city events may interest them as a *partnership*, not spam.
5. **אמהות רמת גן גבעתיים והסביבה** — [facebook.com/groups/849750082958625](https://www.facebook.com/groups/849750082958625/) — covers the Ramat Gan side of your map.
6. **אימהות יפואיות** — [facebook.com/groups/246691485422328](https://www.facebook.com/groups/246691485422328/) — Yafo moms; good coverage test for south TLV events.

### Tier 3 — Verified Facebook groups, English/olim (weeks 4–5, English content is your edge here)

7. **Tel Aviv Parents' Support Group** — [facebook.com/groups/tlvmommas](https://www.facebook.com/groups/tlvmommas/) — English-speaking TLV parents; closest match in the anglo scene.
8. **Secret Tel Aviv** — [facebook.com/groups/secrettelaviv](https://www.facebook.com/groups/secrettelaviv/) — 500k+ members, strict promo rules; time the post well (Sun–Mon morning) and frame as personal story. They also run [WhatsApp communities](https://www.secrettelaviv.com/magazine/blog/useful-info/secret-tel-aviv-whatsapp-community) and an events newsletter — pitching MamaOut to their editorial/City Kids section is a separate, bigger win than one group post.
9. **English speakers in Tel Aviv** — [facebook.com/groups/898281193548526](https://www.facebook.com/groups/898281193548526/).
10. **Olim in Tel Aviv Yafo** — [facebook.com/Olim.Tel.Aviv](https://www.facebook.com/Olim.Tel.Aviv/) — the municipality's page for English-speaking olim; page not group, so this is an outreach/DM target.

### Tier 4 — Communities & orgs (week 6+, partnership rather than posting)

11. **Nefesh B'Nefesh "Bump to Baby"** workshops — new olim moms-to-be; one email to the organizers offering MamaOut as a free resource for attendees.
12. **Peanut app** (TLV area) — English-speaking moms match there; low effort: mention MamaOut in your profile/replies.
13. **Anglo-List** directory — lists 500+ olim Facebook groups; mine it for more targets if tiers 1–3 work: [anglo-list.com/anglo-list-on-facebook](https://anglo-list.com/anglo-list-on-facebook/).

**Rules recap:** read pinned rules first; where promo is restricted, message admins before posting; never post identical text in two groups (Facebook flags it, and members overlap); reply to every comment within 24h.

## Part 2 — The posts

All variants lead with the mom story, not the product. Adjust the italicized bits to be true and current.

### A. WhatsApp — short (EN)

> Hi everyone 👋 I'm on maternity leave with baby #3 and kept losing an hour every time I tried to figure out what to do with her that day — everything's scattered across a dozen Instagram pages. So I built a little site that collects all the mom & baby events in Tel Aviv and Ramat Gan in one place, updated daily. It's free, no signup: [link]
> Would really love to hear if it's useful / what's missing 🙏

### B. WhatsApp — short (HE)

> היי בנות 👋 אני בחופשת לידה עם תינוקת שלישית, ונמאס לי לחפש כל בוקר בעשרה עמודי אינסטגרם מה אפשר לעשות איתה היום. אז בניתי אתר קטן שמרכז את כל האירועים לאמהות ותינוקות בת״א ורמת גן במקום אחד, מתעדכן כל יום. חינם, בלי הרשמה: [link]
> אשמח מאוד לשמוע אם זה עוזר לכן / מה חסר 🙏

### C. Facebook — longer (EN, for tlvmommas / English speakers / Secret TLV)

> **I built a website during maternity leave so you don't have to scroll 10 Instagram pages to find something to do with your baby**
>
> Hi! Mom of three here, currently on leave with a 4-month-old. Somewhere around month two I realized I was spending more time *finding* baby activities than actually going to them — every studio, café and hug-circle posts only on its own Instagram or in some WhatsApp group I wasn't in.
>
> So between naps I built **MamaOut**: it automatically collects mom & baby events across Tel Aviv and Ramat Gan into one place — music classes, baby massage, mom meetups, stroller-friendly stuff — updated every day. Filter by your baby's age, your area, and the day. Free, no signup, works in English and Hebrew: [link]
>
> It's still young and I'm improving it every week, so I'd genuinely love feedback: What's missing? Which places should it cover that it doesn't? 💛

### D. Facebook — longer (HE, for דיגיתף / אמהות רמת גן / אימהות יפואיות)

> **בניתי אתר בחופשת הלידה כדי שלא תצטרכו לחפש בעשרה עמודי אינסטגרם מה לעשות עם התינוק**
>
> היי! אמא לשלושה, כרגע בחופשת לידה עם תינוקת בת 4 חודשים. איפשהו בחודש השני קלטתי שאני מבזבזת יותר זמן על *לחפש* פעילויות מאשר על הפעילויות עצמן — כל סטודיו וכל חוג מפרסמים רק אצלם באינסטגרם או בקבוצת וואטסאפ שאני לא בה.
>
> אז בין שנ״צ לשנ״צ בניתי את **MamaOut**: אתר שאוסף אוטומטית את כל האירועים לאמהות ותינוקות בת״א ורמת גן למקום אחד — חוגי מוזיקה, עיסוי תינוקות, מפגשי אמהות — ומתעדכן כל יום. אפשר לסנן לפי גיל התינוק, אזור ויום. חינם, בלי הרשמה, בעברית ובאנגלית: [link]
>
> האתר עוד צעיר ואני משפרת אותו כל שבוע, אז אשמח באמת לפידבק: מה חסר? אילו מקומות כדאי להוסיף? 💛

### E. Olim-specific angle (EN, for olim groups / NBN)

Same as C, but swap the second paragraph's opening for:

> Extra hard when you're navigating it in your second (or third) language — half the baby activities in this city are announced only in Hebrew, in groups you don't know exist. MamaOut shows everything in English too, automatically translated.

### F. Admin permission message (send before posting where rules are strict)

> Hi! I'm a TLV mom on maternity leave and I built a free, non-commercial site that gathers all the mom & baby events in the city in one place. I think it could be useful for the group but wanted to check with you first — would it be OK to share? Happy to frame it however fits the group's rules.

### G. Reply snippets (for comments)

- "What about [neighborhood/city]?" → *"Not yet — right now it's TLV + Ramat Gan, but tell me where you are, it helps me decide what to add next."* (log every one of these — it's your expansion roadmap)
- "How do you make money?" → *"I don't 🙂 It's a side project from my leave. Maybe someday, but it stays free for moms."*
- "Event X is missing" → *"Adding the source — should show up within a day. Keep them coming!"*
- Any compliment → thank + *"If you know one more group where this would help, feel free to share it 💛"*
