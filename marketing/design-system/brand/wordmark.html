No final logo yet — this is the working direction to iterate from. The name is the promise: mama, out of the house.

Concept: lowercase Fraunces wordmark in plum; the "o" of "out" becomes a butter sun with hand-drawn rays — the one yellow pop.

Casing Always lowercase "mamaout" in the wordmark and handle (@mamaout.app). In running text it's written MamaOut — one word, capital M and O.
Clear space & minimum size Keep clear space equal to the height of the "m" on all sides. Minimum width 90px digital; below that use the sun-dot alone as the mark.
The sun is the mark The butter sun-dot can stand alone as app icon / favicon / avatar: butter sun on plum or pale-lilac circle.
Don'ts
  • No ALL CAPS, no gradients, no outlines
  • Never white text on lilac
  • Never on pure white or saturated purple
  • Don't add strollers, storks or pacifiers to the mark

Open question for the logo session: hand-drawn vs. geometric sun rays, and whether the Hebrew wordmark (מאמאאאוט or Latin kept as-is) is needed — recommendation: keep the Latin wordmark in Hebrew contexts.
