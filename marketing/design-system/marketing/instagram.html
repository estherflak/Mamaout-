Rules

Max 3 colors per post Canvas (cream, pale lilac, or plum) + plum/cream text + one butter accent. That's it.
One doodle per post Sun, squiggle, star, or stroller path — hand-drawn feel, butter or lilac, corner placement. Doodles decorate; they never carry the layout.
Serif headline, sentence case Fraunces ≥ 60pt at 1080px. Kicker and handle in Nunito Sans. Hebrew posts: Noto Serif Hebrew + Assistant, RTL.
No FOMO mechanics No countdowns, no "last spots", no urgency stickers. Content lists what's on; the tone celebrates small wins.
