Text pairings

Plum on cream AA ✓ body
Plum on butter AA ✓ CTAs
Cream on plum AA ✓ fills
White on lilac ✗ never for text
Lilac on cream ✗ decorative only

Rules

Cream or pale lilac canvas Never pure white or saturated purple as a full background.
Butter is earned One yellow pop per composition — the CTA, the sun, the highlight. Not everywhere.
Text is always plum Lilac is for fills, borders and selected states; it never carries text on light backgrounds.
Max 3 colors per Instagram post Cream counts as canvas, not a color.

Migration note: the live app still ships the legacy dusty-rose/sage palette (#d4a5a5 etc. in tailwind.config.js). This palette is canonical; the app migrates toward it. Sage survives only as a functional success color.
