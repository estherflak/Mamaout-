Rules

Sentence case everywhere No ALL CAPS — not in buttons, not in nav, not in captions.
Serif = voice, sans = function Fraunces for headlines and hero text only; everything interactive or dense is Nunito Sans.
Hebrew pairing Assistant replaces Nunito Sans; Noto Serif Hebrew replaces Fraunces. Never mix scripts inside one text block's font.
Numbers and times stay LTR 10:30, 0–8, ₪45 keep their direction inside RTL text.
